As some may have noticed, we've added some translation icons to the website. Right now, they don't do a lot since not much of the website is actually translated, but we will get to them as we can.

If you are able to do translations and you would like to contribute an article translation (or even menu items), we will certainly be interested in them. You can either send them via email directly to me at [striker@lunar-linux.org](mailto:striker@lunar-linux.org), send them to our [mailing lists](http://lists.lunar-linux.org/cgi-bin/mailman/listinfo), or stop by [IRC](irc://irc.freenode.net/lunar) and give us a link.
